## 2. Design contract — typography, color, motion (paste this first)

> **Master system prompt for Claude Design:**
>
> Design system for **Murmur**, a verified anonymous campus Q&A app for Gen Z juniors in India.
> Direction: **warm anonymous community** — approachable, reassuring, gentle, human, low-anxiety;
> "warm like a well-moderated Discord server, softened like Headspace." The UI must feel alive
> and hold attention without ever adding burden: students arrive carrying shame about the
> question they're about to ask, and the interface's job is to make asking feel light.
>
> **Typography:** a rounded, friendly humanist sans for display/headings (the register of
> Nunito, Quicksand, or Baloo 2 — warm terminals, open apertures, never corporate), paired with a
> highly legible neutral sans for body and dense lists (the register of Inter or Plus Jakarta
> Sans). Generous line-height (≥1.5 body), a clear but small scale (one hero size, one heading,
> one body, one caption — students skim on phones between classes), medium weights over bold
> (bold reads as shouting; this product must never shout). Pseudonyms and year badges get a
> slightly playful treatment — they are the user's safe identity and should feel like a friendly
> avatar, not a serial number.
>
> **Color:** warm, low-anxiety palette — soft warm background (warm off-white / pale peach
> undertone), one warm primary (coral/amber family) for primary actions, calm supportive
> secondary (sage or teal) for reassurance states. Hard rules: moderation and safety states are
> **protective, never punitive** — "pending review" renders in calm neutral/secondary tones
> (never yellow-alarm), "blocked" renders in muted warm tones with supportive framing (never
> harsh red), errors are gentle coral, and nothing on any moderation surface may resemble a
> punishment or a warning siren. High contrast for legibility (WCAG AA), dark mode with the same
> warmth (warm dark gray, never pure black).
>
> **Motion:** alive but featherweight — quick springy micro-transitions (150–250ms) on cards,
> buttons, and state changes; a gentle celebratory moment on first-post success and on answer
> accepted; skeleton shimmer for loading (never spinners on content surfaces); no motion that
> delays the user or demands attention. Everything glides; nothing lurches.
